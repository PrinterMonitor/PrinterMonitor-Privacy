# PrinterMonitor Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 9 October 2026**

PrinterMonitor ("the app") lets you monitor and control 3D printers on your own network. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## The short version

- PrinterMonitor does **not** collect, store or transmit your personal data to the developer or to any third party.
- There are **no accounts**, **no analytics**, **no advertising** and **no tracking**.
- Everything the app needs stays on your device, and the app talks only to the printers and cameras that **you** add.

## Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following **locally on your device only**:

- **Printer details you enter:** a name, the printer's IP address or hostname, port number, and an optional camera URL.
- **Printer passwords (optional):** if you enter a password for a printer, it is stored in the system **Keychain**, not in plain text.
- **Your preferences:** for example, temperature presets and the temperature step size.

This information is never sent to the developer. You can delete a printer at any time inside the app, which also removes its saved password. Deleting the app removes the data stored by the app.

## Network connections

The app connects **directly from your device to the printers and camera URLs that you configure**, usually on your local network. It sends the commands you ask for (for example reading status, setting a temperature or starting a print) and receives the printer's responses, files lists, thumbnails and camera images.

- This traffic goes only between your device and your own equipment. The developer cannot see it.
- Many 3D printer controllers use unencrypted HTTP on the local network. Use the app on a network you trust, and do not expose your printers directly to the internet.
- If you enter a camera URL that points to a server on the internet, the app will connect to that address as you instructed. The operator of that server is responsible for how it handles requests.

## Permissions

- **Local Network:** required so the app can reach printers and cameras on your home or workshop network. It is used for no other purpose.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

## Data sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or disclose personal information to anyone. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Backups

Depending on your device settings, data stored by the app may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups) and is then handled under Apple's terms for those services. Keychain items are not synchronised by the app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
